# W005: Buyer and agent channel evidence

State: pending external market and publication evidence; not executed. Depends on: W004 local evidence supporting continuation.

Three independent lanes after a useful prototype exists:

1. Prepare buyer interview questions about the last real integration, time/cost,
   privacy constraints and current alternative; outreach only when authorized.
   Record reports and priced pilot commitments separately from actual payments.
2. Model hosted and BYO costs with measured provider usage and support assumptions;
   decide license, price, service limits and public brand only with evidence.
3. Prepare twelve unbranded agent prompts and a preregistered discovery threshold;
   actual organic discovery scoring needs a publicly accessible product/docs.
   Supplied-doc integration can be tested earlier but is a separate result.

Respect [D003](../decisions/D003-comparison-and-gates.md) gates and
[D004](../decisions/D004-commercial-options.md) limits. Publication, paid model
batches, outreach, pilots and billing are distinct scoped actions; a ready ticket
does not authorize them. No fixed review panel or mandatory external tracker.

Done when buyer, economic and agent outcomes each have evidence or an explicit
pending dependency, and a product/channel decision names residual risks. Do not
manufacture a successful outcome from clicks, mentions or hypothetical revenue.
